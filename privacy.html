Privacy Policy

VelvetVerse Radio respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

When you participate in interactive features such as Discos Pedidos, we collect the information you submit. Streaming providers and other external services may also process technical or usage data under their own policies.

Discos Pedidos — Song Requests

When you submit a request, we collect your name, city, country, requested artist and song, and any optional dedication. We use this information to organise the programme, check for repeated requests, contact-free scheduling and announce requests on air. The requested artist and song may be displayed publicly on the website.

Your name, city, country and dedication may be read aloud during the broadcast, which listeners can hear. Please do not include sensitive personal information or information about other people without their permission in your dedication.

Requests are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and accessed by the radio team for programme management. For questions about your information, or to request correction or deletion, email velvetverseradio@gmail.com. Broadcast content already aired may not be retractable.

Top 5 Latino — Voting

Top 5 Latino voting does not ask for your name or email address. To help limit voting to one vote per edition, the website generates a random visitor identifier, stores it in your browser's local storage, and sends it with your vote and the selected song to Supabase. Your vote and identifier are recorded in the voting database. Voting totals and percentages are displayed publicly, but individual visitor identifiers are not shown in the results.

Deleting browser storage may remove the on-device voting reminder, but it does not automatically delete a previously recorded vote. Service providers may also process technical connection data in order to deliver the website or API.

Comments and Optional Profile Photos

When you submit a comment, we collect the name and comment you provide and, optionally, a URL linking to your profile photo. Comments are reviewed before publication. If approved, your name, comment and any linked photo may be displayed publicly on the Comments page.

Comments and photo links are stored using Supabase. If you provide a photo link, the image may be loaded from an external image provider, which has its own privacy practices. Please use only photos you have permission to share. Contact us if you want to request correction or removal of a published comment or photo link.

Contact Messages

If you contact us by email or through the Contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you send so that we can respond to your enquiry. The website's contact form sends messages through the external FormSubmit service to our email inbox; direct emails are handled by our email provider. We do not publish private contact messages or use them for advertising without permission.

To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent, contact velvetverseradio@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

Cookies

This website may use cookies and browser local storage to support site features, including the Top 5 Latino voting identifier. You can manage cookies and stored site data through your browser settings; removing stored voting data may affect voting controls.

Third-Party Services

We use services including RCast (streaming), Supabase (requests, votes and comments), FormSubmit (contact messages), Apple Music embeds, social media platforms and external image hosts. These services may process technical or submitted information according to their respective privacy practices.

Data Security

Song requests, comments and voting records are stored using Supabase, while contact messages are delivered to our email inbox through the relevant provider. We take reasonable care in handling information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

External Links

Our website contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the website means you accept any updates.
