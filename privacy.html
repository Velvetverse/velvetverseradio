Privacy Policy

VelvetVerse Radio respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

When you participate in interactive features such as Discos Pedidos, we collect the information you submit. Streaming providers and other external services may also process technical or usage data under their own policies.

Discos Pedidos — Song Requests

When you submit a request, we collect your name, city, country, requested artist and song, and any optional dedication. We use this information to organise the programme, check for repeated requests, contact-free scheduling and announce requests on air. The requested artist and song may be displayed publicly on the website.

Your name, city, country and dedication may be read aloud during the broadcast, which listeners can hear. Please do not include sensitive personal information or information about other people without their permission in your dedication.

Requests are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and accessed by the radio team for programme management. For questions about your information, or to request correction or deletion, email velvetverseradio@gmail.com. Broadcast content already aired may not be retractable.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies to improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use external services such as RCast (streaming), YouTube, Spotify, and Facebook. These platforms may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Submitted song requests are stored using Supabase. We take reasonable care in handling this information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

External Links

Our website contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the website means you accept any updates.
