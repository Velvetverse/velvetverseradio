Discos Pedidos — Programme Rules

Song requests are accepted through the Discos Pedidos form, with a maximum of 12 accepted requests for each Sunday programme. Requests received from Monday to Saturday are considered for the next Sunday; requests made on Sunday are considered for the following Sunday. Once 12 valid requests have been received for a Sunday, submissions close for that edition and normally reopen for the next edition.

Requests are handled in the order received, subject to the programme's duration and technical or editorial requirements. Repeated requests for the same song for the same Sunday are not accepted. A submitted request does not guarantee that a song will be broadcast at a specific time or that the programme will always run without changes.

By submitting a request, you acknowledge that your name, city, country and optional dedication may be announced on air, and that the artist and song you requested may be listed publicly on the website. Please keep dedications respectful and do not share sensitive information about yourself or others.
